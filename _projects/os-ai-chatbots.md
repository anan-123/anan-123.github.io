Developed a Follow-Me robot using the Qualcomm RB3 board and wave rover robot, integrating pose estimation
and SLAM for autonomous tracking and navigation. We presented our work at the Qualcomm University Platforms Symposium 2025.
### Key Features:
- **Qualcomm RB3 Robotics Platform**: Leveraged for edge-AI processing.
- **Pose Estimation**: To identify and track the target.
- **ROS 2 (Humble)**: Middleware for modular system integration.
